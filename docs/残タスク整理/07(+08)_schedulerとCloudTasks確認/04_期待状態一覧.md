| 項目 | 期待値 |
|------|--------|

## 2. scheduled job（supervisor 経由）

設定: `storeMeta/schedulerConfig.jobs`（デフォルト: `functions/src/shared/config/schedulerConfigDefaults.ts`）

| jobKey | queue 名 | handler 実行口 | デフォルト schedule | downstream |
|--------|----------|----------------|---------------------|------------|
| `weeklyPlanner` | `scheduled-job-weekly-planner` | `executeScheduledJobTask` → `runWeeklyPlannerTask` | 木 04:40 JST | `business-date-assessment-queue`（open/close） |
| `enqueueTournamentTasksByScheduler` | `scheduled-job-enqueue-tournament-tasks-by-scheduler` | → `runEnqueueTournamentTasksBySchedulerTask` | 毎日 05:00 JST | `tournament-queue` |
| `generateRecurringTournamentsByScheduler` | `scheduled-job-generate-recurring-tournaments-by-scheduler` | → `runGenerateRecurringTournamentsBySchedulerTask` | 木 04:50 JST | Firestore（recurring 生成） |
| `scheduledCleanup` | `scheduled-job-scheduled-cleanup` | → `runScheduledCleanupTask` | 毎日 05:00 JST | Firestore（cleanup） |
| `scheduleGenerateNextYearBusinessHours` | `scheduled-job-schedule-generate-next-year-business-hours` | → `runScheduleGenerateNextYearBusinessHoursTask` | 1/29 05:10 JST | 営業時間生成タスク |
| `payrollNotificationScheduler` | `scheduled-job-payroll-notification-scheduler` | → `runPayrollNotificationSchedulerTask` | 毎日 05:00 JST | Firebase Task Queue `processPayrollNotifications` |
